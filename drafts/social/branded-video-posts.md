## LinkedIn (square)
A brand on a building is a promise.

The only question: who's still keeping it when you sell?

Abu Dhabi now has 31 branded residence projects. Sixth in the world.

But here's the catch:
• 92% of its pipeline has no hotel attached
• Globally, it's 37%
• The average brand premium is about 33%, mostly measured at launch, not at resale

Now compare Miami:
• Big brand in Brickell: from about $5M
• Ritz-Carlton on Al Maryah: from about $1.1M
• Miami property tax on a $2M condo: about $360,000 over 10 years
• Abu Dhabi: zero

Before you pay any brand premium, ask five things:
1. Hotel or no hotel?
2. Who operates it?
3. What does the service charge buy?
4. What's the resale evidence?
5. Who buys it next?

Pass all five, and the premium can be worth every dirham.
Fail two, and you're paying for a logo.
