## LinkedIn (square)
Sold a UK rental for £100,000 profit? You don't keep £100,000.

Here's the 2026/27 maths most landlords never run:
• Gain: £300,000 − £200,000 = £100,000
• Less allowable costs (fees, stamp duty, improvements): £85,000
• Less the £3,000 annual allowance: £82,000 taxable
• Higher-rate CGT at 24%: £19,680

And the part I get asked about most in Dubai: moving to the UAE doesn't make UK CGT disappear. Non-residents still pay UK CGT on UK property, and must report every UK property sale to HMRC within 60 days, even if nothing is owed.

Know your number before you sell. Then put the rest to work.
