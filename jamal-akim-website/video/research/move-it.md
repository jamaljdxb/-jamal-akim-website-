# "Move it here": country fact sheet

Short, checked facts for the "what if we moved Abu Dhabi's fundamentals to…" videos. Each entry: the anchor attraction, the prime areas, and why they cost what they do. Figures come from the sources linked. Agency and broker figures are indicative, so recheck before quoting a number on camera.

## France: the Disneyland effect
- **Disneyland Paris ≈ 6% of France's tourism revenue.** Not GDP. A 2026 SETEC study puts it at about 6.1% of national tourism revenue and links it to around 70,000 direct, indirect and induced jobs. Earlier studies: 6.2% (1992–2012) and 6.43% (2009) of foreign visitor tourism income, plus about €50bn of value added over 20 years. [newsparcs](https://www.newsparcs.com/en/article/00005718/site)
- **Prime Paris:** 6th arrondissement (Saint Germain) at about €14,500–15,800/m², the 7th (Invalides, Eiffel side) at about €14,000–16,000/m², and the 16th (Passy) at about €11,000–13,500/m². The city average is about €10,200–10,600/m². [Home Select 2026](https://homeselect.paris/en/blog/prix-immobilier-paris-2026-analyse-arrondissement/)
- **Why:** museums, river frontage, limited stock of historic buildings, global demand.
- **Line for the video:** "One theme park. Six percent of a nation's tourism money. Now picture a whole cultural district."

## UAE: Ras Al Khaimah
- **Anchor:** Wynn Al Marjan, a $5.1bn integrated resort now slated for 2027. [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/branded-residence-prices-at-al-marjan-island-set-to-double-in-few-years-say-industry-executives)
- **Prime:** Al Marjan Island. Prices went from about AED 550/sqft (early 2022) to about AED 1,100–1,400/sqft (Q1 2026), up 21% year on year. [Oliva](https://joinoliva.com/en/learn/blog/wynn-resort-rak-impact-property-values)
- **Why:** one landmark resort repriced a whole island before it opened. This is the "move one asset" proof in your own backyard.
- **Caveat:** analysts warn current prices may already price the Wynn in, and a delay would push back rental uplift.

## Nigeria: Lagos
- **Prime:** Ikoyi, Banana Island, Victoria Island, and Eko Atlantic (reclaimed land, planned financial district, about ₦4.1m/m², the city's highest). [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/analysis/article/square-metre-eko-atlantic-can-get-plot-mowe/)
- **Ikoyi land:** about ₦1.35m/m² (2022) to about ₦2.45m/m² (2026), up 81%. Demand comes from diplomats, expats, executives and HNWIs. A weaker naira raised rebuild costs, which put a floor under prices. [Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2026/07/10/ikoyi-luxury-real-estate-hits-milestone-as-5-bedroom-homes-cross-n1-1-billion-mark/)
- **Spillover:** Eko Atlantic lifted prices in Victoria Island and Lekki. [The Guardian NG](https://guardian.ng/news/from-broad-street-to-atlantic-city-congestion-asset-depreciation-push-hnis-businesses-to-eko-atlantic-city/)
- **Line:** "Lagos already proved it. Build the infrastructure and money moves in."

## Panama
- **Prime:** Punta Pacifica (oceanfront towers, Johns Hopkins affiliated hospital), Costa del Este (planned, reclaimed land), San Francisco, El Cangrejo. [Expat Exchange](https://expatexchange.com/gdc/9/87/4202/Panama/Real-Estate-in-Panama-City)
- **Why:** canal trade hub, US dollar economy, expat and retiree demand, healthcare.
- **Caveat:** foreigners can buy, but check restrictions near the canal and borders with a local lawyer.

## Mexico
- **Prime:** Polanco (Mexico City's luxury district), plus the Riviera Maya (Tulum, Playa del Carmen) for international buyers. [BuyPlaya](https://buyplaya.com/navigating-tulum-real-estate-a-foreigners-clear-blueprint/)
- **Why:** tourism and lifestyle demand on the coast, and corporate and diplomatic demand in the capital.
- **Caveat:** coastal purchases by foreigners go through a *fideicomiso* bank trust. Sources here are broker marketing, so get local data before quoting prices.

## Philippines: Manila
- **Prime:** Bonifacio Global City (BGC) and Makati. Luxury BGC launches were priced at about ₱430,000/m². [BusinessWorld](https://bworldonline.com/?p=221761)
- **Why:** business district demand, expat rentals (gross yields often quoted at 4–6%).
- **Note:** Entalula Beach (Philippines) is #1 in the 2026 World's 50 Best Beaches, the same list that has Saadiyat at #19. That's a good hook.
- **Caveat:** foreign ownership of condos is capped by building (commonly quoted at 40%). Verify locally.

## Australia: Sydney
- **Prime:** Point Piper, Bellevue Hill, Vaucluse, Darling Point (eastern harbourside). Medians are often quoted at A$7–12m+, depending on source. [Property Tribune](https://thepropertytribune.com.au/market-insights/top-20-most-expensive-suburbs-in-sydney/)
- **Why:** harbour frontage, scarce land, proximity to the CBD, trophy homes.
- **Note:** Wharton Beach (Australia) is #3 on the same beaches list.
