I get asked the same question on almost every call: is now a good time to buy off-plan in Dubai. The honest answer needs numbers, not a vibe. Here's what the data actually says, pulled from the Dubai Land Department and DXB Interact rather than a brochure.

## The scale of the market

Dubai recorded AED 286.4 billion in property sales across 79,229 transactions in H1 2026, according to Dubai Land Department records. Off-plan made up roughly 71% of that volume, continuing a trend that has held for several quarters running.

## Why off-plan keeps leading

Flexible payment plans, investor visa eligibility, and access to new supply in growth corridors are all part of it, but the bigger driver is that overseas investors are increasingly buying for long-term rental income and residency, not just short-term flips.

## The Golden Visa threshold

Property worth at least AED 2 million still qualifies for the 10-year Golden Visa route, based on the Dubai Land Department's official valuation rather than the purchase price. Separately, the two-year property investor visa no longer has a minimum property value for sole owners, which has opened residency up to a wider range of buyers.

## Where I pull this from

I don't rely on brochures or agent claims for market data. Two sources I check constantly:

- DXB Interact (dxbinteract.com), the Dubai Land Department's own public data platform, with real-time transaction history, price trends, and rental yields by community.
- Dubai Land Department (dubailand.gov.ae), the source data itself, including project status and title deed verification.

Numbers like these move every quarter. If you're making a six or seven figure decision, ask whoever you're working with where their numbers actually came from.
