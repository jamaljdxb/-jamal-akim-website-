Before Dubai, before property, there were years in sales, recruitment and financial services. None of it felt directly relevant at the time. Looking back, almost everything I use in property today started there.

## Listening matters more than pitching

The best sales lesson I learned had nothing to do with closing — it was about listening long enough to understand what someone actually needed before saying anything. That's the same skill that matters most in a property conversation now.

## Rejection is data, not verdict

Recruitment in particular teaches you to treat a "no" as information rather than a judgment on you personally. That mindset shift matters just as much when a deal falls through in property as it did on a sales floor.
