Alva is a townhouse launch within Emaar's The Valley community, pitched by the developer as the largest townhouse footprint built in the location to date, with per square foot pricing positioned as the most competitive across Emaar's Dubai communities at launch.

## Pricing and sizes

3BR at AED 4.38M, with BUA around 2,788 sqft and plot around 2,135 sqft, EOI amount AED 100,000. 4BR at AED 5.09M, with BUA around 3,081 sqft and plot around 3,620 sqft, EOI amount AED 150,000.

## Payment plan

Spread evenly at 20% per year across five years: 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029 and 2030.

## Why it's being positioned this way

Per the developer's sales materials, every bedroom in the 3 bed layout is ensuite, which is unusual for the price point, along with a larger than typical entry foyer and the option to close off the kitchen, aimed squarely at end users rather than pure investors. The phase sits alongside mostly independent villas with a high green space ratio.
