SEI Saadiyat sits on Saadiyat Island in Abu Dhabi, one of Aldar's flagship waterfront addresses. The development spans six residential buildings, four mid rise (G+7) and two high rise (G+17), totalling 778 residences.

## Pricing and sizes

Pricing runs as follows. 1BR at AED 2.95M for 70 sqm. 2BR at AED 4.50M for 101 sqm. 2BR plus Maid at AED 5.40M for 137 sqm. 2BR Kanso Loft at AED 7.50M for 151 sqm. 3BR Kanso plus Maid plus Study at AED 8.40M for 208 sqm.

## Payment plan (50/50)

5% booking, then 5% instalments in March and September 2027, 10% instalments through April, December 2028 and August 2029, 5% in April 2030, and the remaining 50% on handover in Q4 2030.

## Amenities

Organised across four themes. Move covers fitness studios, sports courts and a kids' club. Restore covers spa, hot and cold baths and rooftop pools. Connect covers co-working spaces, cinema rooms and a cafe. Breathe covers a discovery trail, gardens and a pets park.
